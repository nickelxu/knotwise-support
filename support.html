Knotwise Support

Knotwise teaches more than 200 knots with step-by-step 3D animations, fully offline.

Using the animations

  • Drag to rotate the knot, pinch to zoom, tap the target button to reset the view.
  • Use the previous and next buttons to move one step at a time, or drag the progress bar.
  • Switch to “360° view” to see the finished knot spin.
  • Tap the mirror button to see the left-handed version.

Safety

Knots can fail. Before relying on any knot where a failure could cause injury, learn from a qualified instructor and always check and back up your knots.
