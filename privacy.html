Knotwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Knotwise is a knot-tying reference app. We built it to work entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. Knotwise does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not send any data to us.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information or Apple ID; the app only checks with the App Store which knots you have unlocked.

Data stored on your device

Your favorite knots and recently viewed knots are saved locally on your device so the app can show them to you. This information never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no information, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
